GradTrack is an AI-powered job application tracker that helps final-year university students streamline their job search and increase their chances of landing their dream job.

AI-powered Job Matching

Receive personalized job recommendations based on your skills and interests, so you can focus on applying to jobs that are a good fit for you.

Personalized Interview Preparation

Get tailored interview preparation and practice to help you feel confident and prepared for your dream job interviews.

Job Application Tracking Dashboard

Track the status of your job applications in one place, so you can stay organized and follow up on your applications in a timely manner.
