GradTrack is an AI-powered job application tracker that streamlines the job search process for final-year university students by providing personalized job recommendations and application tracking.

AI-powered Job Matching

Uses machine learning algorithms to match users with job openings based on their skills, interests, and preferences.

Job Application Tracker

Allows users to track the status of their job applications and receive reminders for follow-ups and deadlines.

Resume and Cover Letter Builder

Provides templates and guidance for users to create tailored resumes and cover letters for specific job openings.
