GradTrack is an AI-powered job application tracker that streamlines the job search process for final-year university students, providing personalized job recommendations and application tracking to increase their chances of landing their dream job.

Job Application Tracker

Track the status of your job applications in one place, and stay organized with our intuitive dashboard. Receive notifications and updates on new job openings and application deadlines, so you can stay on top of your job search and never miss an opportunity.

AI-powered Job Matching

Get personalized job recommendations based on your skills and interests, and focus on applying to jobs that are a good fit for you. Our AI-powered algorithm ensures that you receive relevant job suggestions, increasing your chances of landing your dream job.

Resume and Cover Letter Builder

Create a professional resume and cover letter with our easy-to-use builder tool. Get tips and suggestions from our AI-powered algorithm to improve your application materials and increase your chances of getting noticed by potential employers.

Company Research and Review

Research and review companies to find the best fit for your skills and interests. Our platform provides you with detailed information about companies, including their mission, values, and culture, so you can make informed decisions about your job search.

Interview Preparation Tools

Prepare for your interviews with our comprehensive tools and resources. Get tips and suggestions on common interview questions, practice your responses with our interactive simulator, and increase your confidence and chances of acing your interviews.
