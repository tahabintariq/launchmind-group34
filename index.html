GradTrack is an AI-powered job application tracker that helps final-year university students streamline their job search and increase their chances of landing their dream jobs.

AI-powered Job Matching

Utilizes machine learning algorithms to match students with job openings based on their skills, interests, and career goals.

Job Application Tracker

Allows students to track the status of their job applications and receive reminders for follow-ups and deadlines.

Career Counseling and Feedback

Provides students with personalized career guidance, resume and cover letter review, and interview preparation support.
