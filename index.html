GradTrack is an AI-powered job application tracker that streamlines the job search process for final-year university students, increasing their chances of landing their dream job.

Job Application Tracker

A dashboard to track and manage multiple job applications, helping you stay organized and focused on your job search.

AI-powered Job Matching

An AI algorithm that suggests job openings based on your skills, interests, and preferences, increasing your chances of landing a job that you love.

Resume and Cover Letter Builder

A tool to help you create and tailor your resume and cover letter to each job application, making you a more competitive candidate.
